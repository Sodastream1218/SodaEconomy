# SodaEconomy License FAQ

This FAQ explains the intended application of the SodaEconomy license in common
situations. The authoritative terms are in [`LICENSE`](LICENSE). Trademark and
branding rules are separate and are described in
[`TRADEMARKS.md`](TRADEMARKS.md).

## Is SodaEconomy open source?

No. SodaEconomy is **source available** under the Apache License 2.0 subject to
the Commons Clause License Condition v1.0 and the express permissions stated in
`LICENSE`. Because commercial sale of the software itself is restricted, the
project is not open source under the Open Source Definition.

## May I use SodaEconomy on a commercial or monetized Minecraft server?

Yes. You may use SodaEconomy on private servers, public servers, commercial
servers, large networks, and proxy-backed multi-server environments.

## May I accept donations?

Yes. Donations connected with operating your Minecraft server or network are
permitted.

## May I sell ranks, subscriptions, shop items, or crates?

Yes. Revenue from server access, ranks, subscriptions, shops, crates,
sponsorships, advertising, or similar server operations is permitted. The
license distinguishes revenue from operating a Minecraft server from revenue
obtained by selling SodaEconomy itself.

## May I charge players to access my server?

Yes. Charging for access to a Minecraft server or network that uses
SodaEconomy is permitted.

## May a hosting company include SodaEconomy in a hosting package?

Yes, when SodaEconomy is one component of a broader Minecraft hosting or
managed-server offering. The host must not charge a separate SodaEconomy fee,
sell the JAR, market the service primarily as paid access to SodaEconomy, or
present an unofficial build as official.

## May I sell the SodaEconomy JAR or source code?

No. You may not sell, rent, license for a fee, or otherwise charge for the
SodaEconomy JAR, source code, or another SodaEconomy binary without prior
express written permission from the Licensor.

## May I put the JAR behind a paid download, membership, or paywall?

No. Requiring payment or other consideration to obtain SodaEconomy is not
permitted.

## May I redistribute the unmodified JAR for free?

Yes, provided no fee or other consideration is required, the complete license
and notices are preserved, and the copy is not described as an official
download unless it comes from an official SodaEconomy source. Linking to an
official release is preferred.

## May I modify the source code?

Yes. Private modifications, custom configurations, integrations, and
extensions are permitted.

## May I publish a fork?

Yes, if the fork is distributed without charge and complies with the license.
You must preserve required notices, identify modified files, and follow
`TRADEMARKS.md`. A modified build must use a different primary project name and
must not appear to be an official SodaEconomy release.

## May I sell a fork or a lightly modified premium version?

No. A renamed, lightly modified, or substantially equivalent paid version is a
commercial offering of the software itself and requires prior express written
permission.

## May I use the name “SodaEconomy”?

You may use the name truthfully to refer to the official project, describe
compatibility, provide attribution, or identify the origin of a fork. You may
not use SodaEconomy as the primary name of a modified build, imply official
status, or use official branding in a way that causes confusion. See
`TRADEMARKS.md`.

## May I use an official SodaEconomy logo for my fork?

No. Official logos and branding may not be used to brand third-party or
modified builds. Editorial use in a tutorial, review, or factual discussion is
allowed when it does not imply endorsement.

## May I create tutorials, videos, reviews, or documentation?

Yes. Tutorials, reviews, videos, screenshots, compatibility guides, and
truthful commentary are permitted. Clearly distinguish unofficial material
from official project documentation.

## May I submit a pull request or bug fix?

Yes. Contributions are welcome. By submitting a contribution for inclusion in
SodaEconomy, you agree that it is provided under the same project license and
confirm that you have the rights needed to submit it. See
[`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md).

## Is a separate Contributor License Agreement required?

No. SodaEconomy does not currently require a separate CLA. The contribution
terms in the project license and `CONTRIBUTING.md` apply.

## Where do official downloads come from?

Official downloads are published only through sources explicitly designated by
the SodaEconomy project, including the official repository’s release area.
Mirrors, forks, and third-party builds must be identified as unofficial.
