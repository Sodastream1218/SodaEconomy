# SodaEconomy Trademark and Naming Policy

## Purpose

This policy governs use of the SodaEconomy project name, official logos, icons,
and related project branding. It does not replace or modify the copyright
license in [`LICENSE`](LICENSE).

“SodaEconomy” is the official project name. References to “official marks” in
this policy include the SodaEconomy name and any official logos, icons, visual
identity, or release branding published by the project.

This policy does not state or imply that any mark is registered.

## Uses that are allowed

You may use the SodaEconomy name in a truthful, non-misleading way to:

- refer to the official SodaEconomy project;
- state that a server uses or is compatible with SodaEconomy;
- provide attribution required by the software license;
- publish tutorials, reviews, videos, screenshots, news, or commentary;
- describe a fork as being based on or derived from SodaEconomy; or
- link users to an official SodaEconomy download source.

Such use must not imply sponsorship, endorsement, certification, or official
status when none exists.

## Modified versions and forks

A modified version or fork:

1. must use a different primary project and product name;
2. must clearly state that it is unofficial and modified;
3. may truthfully state that it is based on SodaEconomy;
4. must not use “SodaEconomy” alone, or a confusingly similar variation, as its
   primary name;
5. must not use official SodaEconomy logos or official release artwork as its
   own branding; and
6. must not be presented as an official update, premium edition, successor, or
   authorized distribution.

An acceptable descriptive statement is:

> ExampleEconomy is an unofficial fork based on SodaEconomy.

A modified JAR must not retain packaging, artwork, naming, or download-page
presentation that would reasonably cause users to believe it is an official
SodaEconomy build.

## Official logos and visual assets

Official logos may not be used to brand a third-party build, fork, hosting
product, marketplace listing, or competing economy plugin.

Limited editorial display of an official logo is permitted in a review,
tutorial, comparison, or news report when the context is clear and the use does
not suggest endorsement.

## Official downloads

A download is official only when it is published through a source explicitly
designated by the SodaEconomy project, such as the official repository’s
release area or another project-controlled page identified by the maintainer.

A mirror, fork, repackaged JAR, development build, or third-party distribution
must not be labeled “official.” Third parties should link to the official
release instead of creating a confusing substitute download page.

## Domains, accounts, and marketplace listings

Do not register or use domains, social-media accounts, organization names, or
marketplace listings that are likely to be mistaken for an official
SodaEconomy channel. Descriptive references are allowed when the third-party
nature is prominent.

## No effect on the software license

This policy regulates names and branding only. Permission to use, modify, or
redistribute the software is determined by `LICENSE`. The software license does
not grant a general right to use SodaEconomy trademarks or official branding.
